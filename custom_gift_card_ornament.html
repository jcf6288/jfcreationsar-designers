Size

Finished size: 3.9 x 2.7 in sideways, or 2.7 x 3.9 in upright. Opening: 3.15 x 1.95 in, for a standard 3.4 x 2.1 in gift card. A small hole in the top corner is for a cord or ribbon.

Engraving area: the name line on the bottom edge, about 3 x 0.3 in. Everything else is laser cut.

What you get

Three thin layers of plywood glued together: a back, a pocket layer, and a one-piece cut-out face. The gift card slides into the pocket and shows through the face. Natural wood: grain and color vary from piece to piece.
