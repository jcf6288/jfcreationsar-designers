1. Choose your cover

Will your Bible fit?
These covers hold books up to 7 1/4" wide x 10 1/4" tall x 2" thick. Measure your Bible before you order. The outside is 7 1/2" x 10 3/4", with a handle and a zipper.
Engraving areas: the front pocket is 5 1/8" x 10 1/4", and the back cover is 7" x 10 1/4".
The inside is lined with nylon, has 2 flaps that hold the book in place, and an elastic strap for a pen.

2. Pick a picture for the center

A dark drawing on a light background works best. JPG, PNG or SVG, up to 4 MB. We clean it up and send a proof for your approval.

3. Your name

Spell everything the way you want it engraved. Long names are made smaller to fit.

4. Add a line beside the seam (optional)

It reads from the bottom up, like the verse or name on the sample covers. Short lines look best.

5. Quantity, delivery and price

Price is per cover, before tax. Shipping is a flat $5.00 per order. Ordering for a church group, a class, or a team? Bulk pricing is available for 10 or more. Tell us in the notes and we'll confirm the total with your proof. A needed-by date is a request, not a guarantee.
